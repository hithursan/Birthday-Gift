## 🎯 Overview

A fully interactive **3D solar system birthday website** that takes the viewer on a
cinematic space journey — flying through the solar system, revealing a romantic poem,
and unfolding 7 heartfelt chapters through immersive scroll animations.

## 🎬 Journey Experience

### ⏱️ Intro Animation Sequence
- **`[00 - 02s]`** 🌑 Dark screen loads
- **`[02 - 06s]`** ☀️ Sun appears from darkness
- **`[06 - 10s]`** 🚀 Camera flies into solar system
- **`[10 - 40s]`** 🪐 8 planets revealed one by one
- **`[40 - 52s]`** 📜 Romantic poem appears in gold
- **`[52s +]`** ⬇️ Scroll instruction shows

### 🖱️ Interactive Chapter Scroll
- **`[Scroll]`** 🌌 Camera flies beyond solar system
- **`[Chapter 1]`** 💫 Happy Birthday
- **`[Chapter 2]`** 🌸 Birthday Wishes
- **`[Chapter 3]`** 💌 A Letter From My Heart
- **`[Chapter 4]`** 🎯 My Promises To You
- **`[Chapter 5]`** 💪 You Are My Strength
- **`[Chapter 6]`** 🎂 Make A Wish
- **`[Chapter 7]`** 👑 Forever & Always
